Post-vacation catch-up eval. `v20260719` was auto-trained on 2026-07-19 (one
day after the v20260712 flip) on the standard Sunday cadence, but nobody had
run a comparison against the current main during the vacation window. This is
that comparison.

- **Candidate**: `v20260719` (trained 2026-07-19, cutoff 2026-07-19, n=65,499 rows)

```bash

    --eval-end 2026-07-31 --bootstrap-n 1000
```

## Contrast with the v20260712 promotion decision

When v20260712 was promoted over v20260705 on 2026-07-18, the read was:
5 BETTER / 2 WORSE / 4 TIE — a decisive win on the highest-volume markets
(1X2 home & draw both -3% at p=1.000).

v20260719 vs v20260712 is a **materially different picture**:

- **1X2_home**: was v20260712's biggest win; now no delta (v20260719 didn't
  keep pushing the improvement further).
- **1X2_draw**: was v20260712's second-biggest win; **now regressing** at
  p=0.012. Not catastrophic but a real signal on our top-volume market.
- **AH lines**: was where v20260712 also won 3 of 4; now all 4 tie.
- **OU**: v20260712 tied v20260705 here; v20260719 is significantly BETTER
  on OU — the one place it moves the needle.

The intuition: v20260719 got one more week of training data (65,499 rows vs
63,910) but the incremental data was not obviously more informative on 1X2 —
it may have shifted the model's calibration on draws in an unhelpful
direction. OU is different because OU has been our weakest market historically
(large Pinnacle-close gap) so any improvement there is noticeable.

Which model is closer to the sharp line?

| **under25** | **+9.7% (p=0.000)** | **+12.2% (p=0.000)** | **v20260719 closer by 2.5pp** ✅ |

**The OU-vs-Pinnacle gap tightening is the most interesting number in this
eval.** v20260712 sits at +12.2% off Pinnacle on OU; v20260719 shaves that to
+9.7%. That's 2.5pp of sharp-alignment — larger than the entire spread we saw
between v20260712 and v20260705 on 1X2 (which we promoted for).

This is why we currently run `v20260621` as a dedicated OU override — the main
models have historically been far from Pinnacle on OU. If v20260719 closes
2.5pp of that gap, it might beat v20260621 too, which would let us retire the
override.

Head-to-head `v20260719 vs v20260621` on OU is the natural next test (running
now: `dev/active/rigorous-eval-v20260719-vs-v20260621-ou.log`).

## Recommendation

**Do NOT promote v20260719 as main.**

Reasons:
1. 1X2_draw regression at p=0.012 on our highest-volume market. 1.8% log-loss
   is meaningful over the volume we run.
2. No offsetting 1X2 gains (home is a tie, away is a non-significant "better").
3. AH lines lost the gains v20260712 had over v20260705.
4. Only significant improvement is on OU — a market we already have a
   dedicated override for.

**But run v20260719 vs v20260621 on OU.** If v20260719's OU predictions beat
v20260621's, we can retire the override, simplify the stack, and let the newer
bundle handle both markets. If v20260621 still wins, keep the status quo.
