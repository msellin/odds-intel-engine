### inplay_n deep-dive (the one structural fix recommendation)

Strategy at `workers/jobs/inplay_bot.py:2635` requires `pm_home_prob >= 0.62` OR `pm_away_prob >= 0.62` to trigger. In low-data leagues the ensemble model returns inflated probabilities that don't match the market. Example bets:

| Match | Picked | Our model | Market implied | Live @ pick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| St. Anna v Wohnbau Dietach | home | **83%** | 35% | 7.7% (13.00) |
| Mbarara v SC Villa | home | **66%** | 19% | 13% (7.50) |
| Albion FC v Wanderers | home | **70%** | 47% | 22% (4.50) |

Model says 70-83% favorite; market said 35-47% pre-match; live odds drifted to 7-22%. The bot logic ("favorite drifted, take favorite") is sound, but the input "favorite" is wrong for these leagues. Result: 12.5% hit rate, −50.6% ROI.

**Recommended fix** (~10 lines, one if-statement):

```python
# After line 2688 in _check_strategy_n, before live-odds drift check:
# Sanity gate: if our model says ≥0.62 but the market pre-match implied
# the same side at < 0.50, the model is the one likely wrong (low-data
# league overconfidence). Skip.
market_pre_implied = float(pm.get(f"prematch_implied_{fav_side}") or 0)
if 0 < market_pre_implied < 0.50:
    return None
```

Requires verifying `prematch_implied_home/away` is in `pm` dict (it should be — populated by `_get_prematch_data`).

## Status legend

- 🟢 **KEEP** — Active, profitable, healthy CLV, large enough sample. Scale if possible.
- 🟡 **MONITOR** — Profitable but small sample / recent trend uncertain. Don't change, re-evaluate after more data.
- 🟠 **FIX** — Identified logic issue or persistent loss with addressable cause. Do specific fix.
- 🔴 **RETIRE** — Confirmed structural loser. Negative CLV or 0-win combo. Stop placing.
- ⚪ **TOO-SMALL / DORMANT** — Insufficient data to evaluate or not placing recent bets.
