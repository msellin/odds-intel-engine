# #191 audit, part 2 (2026-09-26, read-only)

**Method.** Each bot is described from `bot_config` (the exported real config), `bot_ledger` and the writer code, not from the docs.
- **Admin figures** come from `bot_performance` (`roi_own`, `clv_own`): the numbers /admin/bots shows (§86). They cover in-record legs only.
- **Independent CLV** is `odds_own × p_close_cons − 1`, taken from `leg_clv_sharp` where `cons_status='ok'`. That is a close from 5 or more books, with Pinnacle and the pick's own book excluded (§85).
- **Estonian re-price (the #172 method)** re-prices each leg at the best quote from ACCESSIBLE_BOOKMAKERS. The books are Coolbet, Epicbet, Tonybet and Unibet-Site. A quote counts only if it is at or before pick time, at most `ODDS_FRESH_MAX_MIN` old and anchor-sane. It is computed with `scripts/analysis/own_track_estonian_reprice.py`'s `fetch_quotes`/`reprice` functions, restricted to these bots.
- **Confidence intervals** come from a bootstrap by match (10,000 resamples, seed 191), reported as 95% CIs.
- **Pre-W2.1 caveat.** Shadow legs picked before 2026-09-25 15:22 UTC are priced at `own_basis='pick_time_snapshot'`. Where no snapshot survived, the basis is `our_books_unverified`, and the count is given.
- **In-play** has no closing line. It is judged on lift (hit-rate minus the book's de-vigged probability) and on ROI against break-even.
- **"Overlap"** is the share of the bot's distinct (match, market, selection) legs that another bot also picked. "/perf" means any of the 10 ACTIVE or TESTING bots.

## Notes

1. **"Settings unknown" on `bot_ah_sharp_v1`: the exporter entry is not missing.** `scripts/export_bot_config.py` got its AH block (lines 415–432) in the same commit as the bot (84aca317). A dry `build_rows()` produces a full row for it: sharp_generator, simulated_bets, 10 AF books, 7 gates. The `bot_config` table has no row because the export runs only at 03:40 UTC (`workers/scheduler.py:3601`), and the bot was created at 16:33 UTC. The scheduler comment admits "on-deploy is not wired yet". `bot_own_1x2_v1`, created at 15:19 UTC today, is in the same state.
   - **Fix, not applied:** run `python3 scripts/export_bot_config.py` once on the VPS. This is a one-off DB write.
   - **Structural fix:** also run the export on scheduler start-up (i.e. on every deploy restart), next to `bot_status.sync_rule_versions`.
2. **The consensus_d admin record is misleading.** `bot_ledger.in_record` counts forward-test legs only if sent or rechecked. Grade D is never sent, so every v2 D leg (46 since 09-24) is excluded, and the row sits at 10 settled legs from 09-22/23. This happens whichever verdict is chosen, and any never-sent arm would have the same problem if it were graded.
3. **Pinconf is not a subset of its parent in practice.** It holds all 77 parent legs since 09-25 12:00, plus 11 that the parent could not claim. Those 11 were already held by another arm: consensus c/d before 09-25 12:00, or the sharp, aligned or junk arms (cross-arm de-dupe). The Pinnacle-confirm gate has not vetoed a single leg, so a twin-vs-parent readout at n = 100 would read ≈ 0 by construction.
4. **The model-bot fleet has no bot on the latest model outside /perf.** `bot_combined_1x2_v1` is the only one on r1x2_comb_v1, and it duplicates `bot_v10_1x2_newplus_v1`. Every other MODEL bot here runs a stale head: v20260712, v20260830 or r1x2_d8plus_v1. The OWN 1X2·MODEL slot should therefore be *new* (r1x2_comb_v1, priced at the best Estonian book, research-first per CLAUDE.md), not an inherited one.
5. **Price caveats.**
   - 19 of 22 (coolbet_1x2), 33 of 33 (coolbet_ou) and 288 of 461 (unified_gate) settled legs are pre-W2.1. Of these, 8, 19 and 41 respectively have no surviving pick-time snapshot (`our_books_unverified`); dropping them changes no verdict.
   - Forward-test arms carry the published price, which the W2.1 rewrite never touched.
   - Estonian re-price coverage is limited by the 7-day snapshot retention (§59) for legs older than about 09-19.
6. **Registry drift worth fixing with the retirements.** `bot_unified_gate_1x2_paper_v1` has a placement path (`placement_gate`, `coolbet_placer_bots` row, toggle OFF), while `bot_registry.py` and SYSTEM_MAP say "never placeable". Retiring it closes this.
